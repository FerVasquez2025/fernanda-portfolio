SURA · Digital ecosystem rebrand UX Research · UX/UI · Design System

Reimagining a fragmented digital ecosystem.

I was brought in to help rethink SURA’s digital experience across multiple business units, combining user research, information architecture, UX redesign and a refreshed visual system into a more consistent and scalable ecosystem.

The challenge

Multiple businesses. Multiple experiences. One brand.

SURA’s digital ecosystem had evolved across several business units, each with its own navigation, information structure and visual patterns.

User research showed that people often struggled to understand where to find services, which platform they needed to use, and how different SURA properties related to one another.

01

Fragmentation

Different SURA properties used distinct navigation patterns, page structures and interaction models.

02

Findability

Users described difficulty locating specific services, understanding categories and determining which site to use.

03

Inconsistency

Pages looked and behaved differently across the ecosystem, increasing cognitive load and weakening the sense of one connected brand.

My role

From research to a new digital language.

I worked across the redesign process, combining research, experience strategy, information architecture, wireframing, visual exploration and system documentation.

Discovery

Understanding how people actually navigate SURA.

The discovery phase started with a research plan and interviews with 10 users to understand how people interacted with SURA’s websites, how they searched for services, and where they became confused or frustrated.

Interview findings were synthesized through empathy mapping and journey mapping to identify common usability issues and recurring expectations.

Insight 01

Users struggled to understand where a service lived.

Several users expected to find everything inside one SURA experience instead of having to understand the company’s organizational structure first.

Insight 02

Too much information made simple tasks feel harder.

Dense content, long pages and inconsistent hierarchy increased the amount of scanning required to complete basic tasks.

Insight 03

Users expected one recognizable SURA experience.

Differences between sites made users question whether they were still inside the same ecosystem.

Information architecture

Designing the ecosystem before designing the screens.

The existing experience contained multiple business units, services and resource areas. Mapping those relationships made it possible to create clearer hierarchies and more predictable navigation patterns.

Design evolution

Structure first. Visual language second.

The redesign evolved progressively from layout and information hierarchy into a fully branded digital experience.

Rebrand foundations

Building a more expressive SURA.

The visual direction expanded the brand into a digital language using clearer hierarchy, expressive color, illustration and a reusable foundation for multiple business units.

Color

A broader palette

A flexible palette allowed different business lines to feel distinct while still belonging to the same visual system.

Typography
Aa

Heading

Illustration

Human-centered imagery

Illustration introduced warmth and made complex insurance, health and service concepts easier to approach.

Design system

From visual direction to reusable components.

The redesign was supported by documented patterns and component guidance to help teams apply the system consistently across different digital products.

Navigation

Anatomy, states and usage guidance for global navigation.

Tabs

Variants, selected states and anatomy documentation.

Checkboxes

Size, states, anatomy and usage recommendations.

Wizard

Progress states and guidance for multi-step flows.

Buttons

Primary, secondary and tertiary action patterns.

Final experience

One ecosystem. One visual language.

The redesigned experience brought SURA’s different properties into a more cohesive digital system while preserving the needs and identity of each business unit.

Outcome

A more coherent foundation for SURA’s digital products.

The project connected research, information architecture, rebranding and system thinking into a common digital language that could scale across multiple SURA properties.
